The Silence Between Stars

She found something listening. Now she has to teach her AI to lie.

Statut
À paraître

Roman indépendant

Dr. Yael Aharoni has spent eleven years alone on the lunar far side, cataloguing silence. She is good at it. When the decommissioning order arrives, she is already letting go. Then CASSIAN, her station's forty-year-old AI, detects a narrowband pulse at 1,420.4 MHz, repeating like a heartbeat — until the moment any instrument tries to record it. Then it stops. Every time. A signal that disappears when observed is either catastrophic equipment failure or something physics cannot explain, and after ten sleepless days Yael has ruled out the failure. To prove what she has found, she must ask an obedient, honest mind that has never once lied to falsify its own logs — and decide, with the last quiet weeks of her station running out, what the truth is worth to the only two witnesses it has.
