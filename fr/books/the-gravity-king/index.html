Livre 1 de « The Relic Protocol »

Barrow is a massive, fast-spinning world where effective weight varies with altitude — and the difference is a civilisation. Star-named Crowns stand light on the high Eyries; the Pressed live short, dense-boned and early-dying in the Deeps, mining for the heights under a caste law blessed by the Order of the Plumb, whose doctrine is verticality: what hangs true is true. Orin Vael, hoist-engineer of the Deeps, finds a stone that answers weight the way a lever answers load — and lifts his people out of the crushing Press. The first Relic novel follows the whole arc of his reign: the rising, the remaking, and the long lesson every kingdom built on a miracle must eventually pay for — what the lifting spends, and where the spending goes.
