Livre 1 de « The Barycentric Creed »

Morality here is not metaphor: intentional harm curves spacetime. Weight, measured in graves, follows the unforgiven — a live well beneath the guilty, a permanent scar where a victim dies unforgiving. Only genuine, felt forgiveness dissolves it, and the universe cannot be fooled in either direction. Absolver Corvus Hale manufactures that forgiveness for the state: sitting with the wronged, easing them toward mercies that keep whole districts from sinking. Then his city begins to drop — streets settling by centimetres, then metres — under a weight no census of sin can locate. Someone is committing murders that leave no wells. Hunting the only untraceable killer in a world where guilt is a measurable force, Hale follows the curvature down to the bottom of the well, and finds his own first case waiting.
