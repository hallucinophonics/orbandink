Livre 1 de « The Seventh Watch »

Karsk at the height of its cycle: an empire of water law and aquifer temples, barrow-fields and survey corps, under a dying empress auditing forty years of reign for the sin that summoned judgment. The Vespers — the robed order of every apocalypse myth, the ones who arrive at the end — have come early, and they have no explanation. The hour is the hour. The court splits over obedience; the survey corps rides for the Edge Mountains that scripture calls the end of the world; and a Vesper novice begins to suspect that his order's liturgy is a technical manual nobody has read correctly in a very long time. The opening movement of a six-book epic that keeps its greatest secret exactly where scripture keeps all of them: in plain sight, in the oldest words.
