The Class Action

Forty-four manufactured civilisations. One class. One dead defendant.

Livre 2 de « Terms & Conditions »

Two years after the recall notice, humanity has survived its ninety days and learned what it survived them for. The recall is stayed on conditions — the first being that proceedings shall progress — and Rita Kessler of Mercer Falls, Ohio, is now the most reluctantly famous person alive: the Adjuster, delivering notarised apologies door to door while the galaxy watches the docket. Her next filing is the largest in the history of matter: the first interstellar class action, organising every manufactured civilisation into one suit against the estate of their maker. Rita wins her way toward the only verdict worse than losing. Under the law she herself chose, against an insolvent estate there is exactly one remedy: to inherit it — assets, liabilities, and the debts of God.
