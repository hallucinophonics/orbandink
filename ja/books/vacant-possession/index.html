「The Night Tenant」第2巻

The flare, again — from the other side of the Latch. In Vera Lin's emergency room, mid-handover casualties scream in two voices; a ward of residue patients recite fragments of jobs they ran asleep; a woman asks for the day husband the Board lists as her co-tenant — who has never existed. Vera kept nine stolen minutes, not six. She saw the empties collecting canisters from the hospital's vault feed. And she took the photograph — from inside her own flat, of the figure already standing there. The second Night Tenant novel runs the mirror first — every planted detail of book one recontextualised from the night side — then drives past the ending it shares, into custody disputes over single bodies, forecasts of a season-long storm, and the Board's quietest ledger of all.
