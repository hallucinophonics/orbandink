「The Seventh Watch」第2巻

The Sound: reef-cities and tide-farms, current-guilds under the Lady of Currents, and the choir — vast gentle deep-fauna whose infrasong is weather, omen and scripture at once. When the tide-tables break, the retreating coast uncovers vault-fields and stairs never meant to be seen dry, and tide-singer Teo Lien is commissioned to find where an ocean goes. His expedition runs parallel to a distant desert war that arrives here as rumor, then refugee, then army — and it sails toward the storm-trench at the world's edge, where the answer to the falling sea has been waiting, patient as engineering, since before the Sound had names. The wonder-voyage of the sequence: a book about reading water, written for readers who assemble the timeline themselves.
