The Clockmaker's Tide

When the tide-clocks stop, the sea demands answers—and one clockmaker must pay the price.

単独作品

Elara Voss has spent decades repairing Drowned Reach’s tide-clocks, machines that predict drownings with unerring precision. But when every clock halts at 3:17 a.m., the town’s oldest resident vanishes, leaving behind a journal that implicates Elara’s late wife in a decades-old mystery. The discovery of Mira’s ledger—filled with cryptic entries about a debt the sea demands—unravels everything Elara thought she knew. As the town’s secrets rise like the tide, she must confront a horrifying truth: the clocks don’t just predict drownings. They enforce them. With the sea’s ledger in her hands, Elara must choose between honoring a pact that has bound her town to the ocean for generations—or breaking it, no matter the cost.
