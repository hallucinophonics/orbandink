The Ingathering

「The Seventh Watch」第5巻

The world was re-plumbed by design so that no realm's life-support is complete alone: Karsk drinks only if the Sound's locks open, the Sound forges nothing without Kiln metal, everyone eats from Solace. Now every realm is awake together, and the age's three great answers go to war — the Restoration, who would repair the old order by force and whose theology is sincere; the Breakers, who refuse arrival itself; and the Compact, gathering keys across five books of consequence. The all-fronts book of the Seventh Watch writes logistics as battle and battle as logistics: sieges are plumbing, supply is doctrine, and the tempering question of the whole sequence — what pressure is for — is argued in the open at last, and never resolved cheaply.
