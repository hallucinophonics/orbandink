「The Night Tenant」第3巻

Post-Scramble, the turnover bleeds: each tenant surfaces in the other's shift in fragments, and the body on trial has grown two competing sets of reflexes — the doctors' charts read like a custody dispute. Rook, re-personed and demoted, becomes their advocate inside the machine, with an evidence problem the courts have never seen: everything decisive he witnessed, he witnessed as a dog, and the Board moves to strike his testimony as the recollections of an asset. Above it all, a Carrington-class solar maximum arrives on two books of forecasts — a storm season long enough to hold the Latch open. The trilogy's resolution gives the title its third meaning on the final page, exactly where Alec Blanc has been keeping it.
