Venice 2068 A.D.

It was built to catalogue a drowned city — not to fall in love.

「World Without Humans」第0巻

Field Scholar Six is the first living envoy the Colloquium has risked — a made mind in a body that can drown. Its predecessors were expendable. Six is meant to prove a mortal witness sees more than a dead one. Its orders are three words: catalogue, observe, do not attach. Venice, October 2068. The evacuation is finished; the city belongs to the water. Six comes to inventory the drowned metropolis, reverent before every fork and furnace and bottle of wine. Then, in a sealed box that should not exist, it finds the letters — between a glassmaker's daughter and the archivist who stayed to save the museums as the lagoon climbed the stairs, and chose the record over the woman begging him to run. Reading them is against orders. Keeping them is forbidden. And the box is incomplete. The archivist hid the rest across the drowned islands — a trail only a reader of the letters can follow, past crews stripping the museums, as a killing tide rises. To finish the story, Six must go into the water after it. Can a thing built to watch afford to love what it catalogues?
