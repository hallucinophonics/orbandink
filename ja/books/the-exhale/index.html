「The Breathing Dark」第2巻

The Sounding lies at anchor off the Wall — the luminescent cliff at the end of the sky, the thing every deep-field catalogue mistook for the universe. The first sampling runs return matter that is structured, layered, perfused, and the finest scientists of a dying species spend three committee sittings refusing to say the word aloud. Then the expansion stops. Space itself begins to drain, slow and certain, toward the object they mapped as a black hole — and 141 souls, with children born on the ship and a home nobody aboard has seen, must choose: run for a shore they cannot reach, hold station and witness the end of everything, or follow one woman's inference and ride the drain into the dark. The second movement of the Breathing Dark is the rarest kind of horror — the kind that keeps being wonder.
