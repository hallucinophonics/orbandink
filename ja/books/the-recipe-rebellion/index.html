「The Universal Menu」第2巻

Fourteen months after the landing, Earth is in full bloom: the cures work, the lifespans climb, the volunteer lotteries oversubscribe — and Max Reznik, the man who told his species the truth, is a stale meme. The Hospitality found his warning charming: a Master's passion for his ingredient honours the table. So when Earth's entry hearings convene at the Concourse, the seat of the Guild of Critics, Max goes — and finds, in the service corridors of alien politics, a rebellion nine centuries old against the eaters of worlds. He stakes Earth on it. His masterstroke ends the war. Earth is the peace treaty. The middle course of the Universal Menu is Bianco's satire at its sharpest: a book about reading the room, in a room where you are the menu.
