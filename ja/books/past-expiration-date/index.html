「The Universal Menu」第3巻

Year three: the year of the Proof. The Grand Service arrives over five months — restaurants the size of city districts stationing above Earth's capitals, cloche hulls glowing the colour of a warming lamp — and Earth is festive and terrified, mostly festive. The capitals compete for compliments; the Cleanse is declared humanity's healthiest year, the cures now conditional on compliance. Max Reznik and Ruth Kessler front the only opposition on the planet, a campaign that is a farce by construction — two middle-aged professionals against the best banquet in the galaxy. The last card left to a line cook destroyed by a review is the kitchen's oldest right, and the trilogy's last course plays it exactly as written: to the table, in front of everyone, with the whole species watching the critic's fork.
