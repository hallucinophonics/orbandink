Libro 2 de The Calorate

On the rim world of Vesh, they burn people for warmth. Veya Marn learned the arithmetic of empire on a world going dark vein by vein — one district, one kettle, one brother at a time. When the Gradient asked for her by name, she did not run. She surrendered. Now she stands on Aurentine, crown world of administered noon: mirror-gardens, snow rooms, sherbets of true comet-ice — and, twelve leagues out, a sea that has quietly begun to steam. The Church calls her a saint. The court calls her a relic. Both have a name for what she is — the Candle who wouldn't burn — and neither will say aloud that a relic is only a thing you keep until you need it. Veya can nudge the flows. She can bloom frost across a throne-room floor and give this court a very fine miracle. What she cannot do is hide where the cost lands. So she makes them watch it land. Far below the gardens, an auditor closes the quarter's books and cannot make four degrees reconcile. Paradise is warm because somewhere, someone is not. What is owed to the warm?
