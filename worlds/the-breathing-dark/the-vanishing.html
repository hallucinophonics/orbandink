Book one · 377 pages

2098. A star in Cepheus is present on Tuesday and gone on Thursday.

No explosion, no remnant, no model. Iva Lenart spends seven years being right alone while the count climbs past a thousand. Then Deneb fades before a hemisphere’s held breath, and the price of humanity’s only expedition is her signature on a comfortable lie. She signs. Twenty years into the dark, to learn where the stars have gone.

From the catalogue · Entry 0902

Two plates of one patch of sky.

Her craft is noticing absence. Every star humanity ever weighed still burns. Only the weightless go out.

Six ways in.

Three to read, three to hear. None has a date yet; each becomes a button here the day it is out.
