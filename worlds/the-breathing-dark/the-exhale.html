The dark was never empty. It was breathing.

Book two · 363 pages

A pause like the top of a breath. Then the sky reverses.

At the far edge of the sky, the instruments agree on something impossible: the expansion of the universe has slowed. Then stopped. Blueshift rises like a tide, and the expedition that crossed twenty years of dark is standing in the path of the answer, with three ways out: run for a home no sum can reach, stand and transmit to the very end, or ride toward the one thing on the map that might be a door.

Six ways in.

Three to read, three to hear. None has a date yet; each becomes a button here the day it is out.
