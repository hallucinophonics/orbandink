The book

Anselm Serle, Actuary Second Grade, computes the schedule that keeps Meridian certain: every guaranteed outcome paid for with a misfortune shipped downriver. He is precise, incurious, and very good at his job. Then a file returns to his desk marked reconciliation failure: a nine-year-old scavenger has survived three fatal accidents he calculated, certified and stamped. The investigation will take him across the river, toward a child the machine cannot kill.
