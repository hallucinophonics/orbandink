The book

Two years after the world’s luck came unpinned, cause and effect have stopped being colleagues. Harvests are drawn by lottery. Skill is decoupled from result. Into this weather come three offers to pin the world again — each sincere, each monstrous. The last actuary, the girl fate cannot touch, and a daughter raised in perfect certainty must decide whether the long honest chaos is worth what it eats. Randomness is not justice. It is merely not rigged.
