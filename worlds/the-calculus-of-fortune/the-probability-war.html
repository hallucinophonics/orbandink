The book

The Gantries are occupied: Adjusters on every corner, carrying folders instead of rifles. In cellars along the east bank, the resistance has learned the machine’s own secret — every schedule of misfortune carries a return address. A Probability Mirror posts the bad luck back to the people who signed it. The weapon is doubt, and it works too well. At the centre: a twelve-year-old girl the engines cannot find, learning exactly what it costs to aim.
