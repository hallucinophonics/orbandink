She read all forty-four thousand pages. Then she found the box.

Presidents hold summits. Generals mobilise fleets. Rita Kessler prints the first two hundred pages and takes them home to a Formica kitchen table, and starts reading at page one. Eleven days later she files a clarifying question about clause 882(c), in proper form, by registered mail, and keeps the receipt. Under the Response Doctrine, whoever responds substantively to a served instrument becomes counsel of record for the respondent. Rita Kessler is now the legal representative of her species. Her opponent has been unreversed on appeal for eleven thousand years.

Read it. Hear it. See it.
