What does winning cost?

The endgame is a single proceeding, broadcast to every world in the arm. The third door is found where everything in this story is found: in the fine print, by someone who reads it for pleasure. Four words from a billion-year-old conveyancing schedule, as-is, where-is, assemble into the largest emancipation ever argued. And Rita Kessler knows that no one has ever read an order all the way through on the day they won it. She starts that night.
