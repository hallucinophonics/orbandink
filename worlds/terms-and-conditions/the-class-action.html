Earth was one of many.

The Production Run Manifest lists every world the Manufacturer ever seeded, and its dispositions column reads like a claims ledger for civilisations: RECALLED. COMPLETED. VOID. PENDING. Rita Kessler writes three words on a yellow legal pad and turns it round: batch claims consolidate. What follows is the first interstellar class action, manufactured species versus the estate of their maker, organised one impossible client at a time, before a court in session for eleven thousand years, where standing is taken literally and everything filed is heard in full.
