They rated her four-tenths and set her aside. It was their first mistake. On Vesh there is no weather, only maintenance: the world's core was tapped centuries ago, and the linewalkers keep the last lines open by hand so the town does not die in the dark. Then the tithe assay comes down, and Veya Marn's brother Joren tests true. Veya does not accept the arithmetic. She stows away on the barge carrying the year's Candles up out of the cold.
