THE ENTRY · SEALED UNTIL RELEASE

Aurentine has never known a cloud. The crown world's summer is administered, its tides are scheduled, and its aristocrats smuggle cold. Into this paradise comes a woman from a world that froze to pay for it. She makes frost bloom across the Noon Palace floor — and makes sure everyone watching learns exactly where the cost of a miracle lands. And beyond the pleasure-piers, very quietly, the sea has started to steam.
