The deep has started answering letters. Every reply knows things only the dead knew.

Weeks after the transmission, the dialogue begins. Letters that once took generations are answered in days, and each reply is authenticated by intimacies no living person could know: a burned confession, a childhood name, the precise hour of a miscalculated descent. Authentication by intimacy; horror by the same. Liss Amaranth, the only person the choir addresses by name, must decide what the Reach owes its dead, and whether what answers is still them. The duology holds its double reading, transcendence or extinction, to the final page.

Heaven is the horizon.
