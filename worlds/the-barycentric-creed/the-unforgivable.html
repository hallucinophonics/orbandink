Book III's description follows from the end of Book II.

It is the year of the Passage, and the Great Absolution is the most beloved thing the state has ever done: complete healing, free, for every absolution veteran. Tessa Orn, a ferry-master's widow woven years ago to forgive the pilot who drowned her husband, travels the ferry roads to have the last of it eased. The reader who has come this far knows what the gift is. She does not.
