The record

On Orison, the ground keeps books: a murder weighs one grave, and the house it happened in sinks. Odran Krast, First Weaver of Clemency, has manufactured forgiveness several hundred times and can no longer name a real feeling in himself. Now the Guard brings him murders it cannot pattern, each scar exactly one grave, and a trail that ends at the bottom of a well he has spent twenty-five years refusing to look into.
