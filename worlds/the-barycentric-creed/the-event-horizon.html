The record

The drift a generation of surveyors filed as instrument error resolves, occultation by occultation, into an object: dark, silent, planet-grade, first lethal pass in three years. It cannot be deflected; weight cannot be pushed. On a world whose constitution, physics and conscience all forbid harm, every door closes in turn. And Odran Krast, the most trusted man on Orison, begins, slowly and in good faith, to prove to himself that what the world needs, nobody else can do.
