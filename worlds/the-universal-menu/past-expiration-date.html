Universal Menu, Earth entry, margin note

The courseServed third

Year three. Restaurants the size of city districts station above Earth's capitals, and the planet is festive and terrified, and mostly festive. Mandatory is declared; humanity's healthiest, happiest year begins. Max Reznik fronts the only opposition on Earth: two middle-aged professionals begging their species to stay polluted, against free healthcare. But a cook destroyed by one review still knows the kitchen's oldest right. It will be the most important cooking demonstration in human history, and it must be excellent.

Tasting notes

  • Petrol-bright on the surface; something turning underneath.
  • The kitchen's oldest right, plated for a full house.
  • Best served spoiled. That is the gambit; it is in the title.
