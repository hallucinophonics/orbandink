The courseServed first

First contact happens between the dumpster and a Superfund lake, while Max Reznik is taking out the trash. The visitors ignore the helicopters and bow to the man who smells of the griddle. They name him Master of Matter Preparation and invite him aboard a ship that is utopian in every particular but one. The gift centres are laid out like prep stations. The medical scans grade like quality inspections. And the banquet in his honour has a menu nobody on Earth has thought to read.

Tasting notes

  • Notes of chrome, diesel, and a kindness held one beat too long.
  • A utopia flawless in every particular but one.
  • Finishes clean. Then does not finish at all.
