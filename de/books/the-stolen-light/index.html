Band 2 von The Relic Protocol

Sixty-four centuries after Barrow shed itself into the sky, the Vesper Chain — stations strung round a dying red dwarf — runs on light. Coherence is wealth: information travels as light, factories fabricate with it, food grows under it. The rich buy coherent beams; the poor live in Scatter, under second-hand glow the coherent have already used once. Coherence is wealth is not a proverb on the Chain; it is a tariff schedule. Senna Vail is a coherence-runner, a thief in a mirror-lined coat, and the job is simple: one black cube, out of the oldest vault in the Chain. What she catches from it makes her the first person in the universe to carry a dying god — station to station, with the darkness closing behind her exactly as fast as she rests.
