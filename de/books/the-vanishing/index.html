Band 1 von The Breathing Dark

In 2098, Iva Lenart works difference imaging at a survey data centre on the Adriatic; her craft is noticing absence. One night the pipeline flags a twelfth-magnitude star in Cepheus — present in the reference frame, gone in the new one, a 36-hour fade with no explosion, no remnant, no model. The verdict is pipeline artefact. Iva spends seven years being right in everyone else's models while the count climbs: five, four hundred, fourteen hundred. The pattern she finally proves is the strangest fact in the history of astronomy — the vanishings have a shape, and the shape is closing. To buy humanity its only expedition into the dark, she trades away the truth of what she found; to see it arrive, she rides twenty years into a sky going progressively blind, toward the place the stars have gone.
