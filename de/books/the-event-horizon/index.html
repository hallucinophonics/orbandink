Band 2 von The Barycentric Creed

The drift a generation of surveyors filed as instrument error resolves, occultation by occultation, into an object: dark, silent, planet-grade. It is the remnant of Ereth — the world the founders' genocide collapsed into a black hole — nine millimetres of horizon crowned by the tumbling debris of everything they fled. Its path crosses Orison's in three years. A civilisation built on the physics of forgiveness now faces a problem forgiveness cannot curve away: every survival plan requires an act that would sink its maker beyond retrieval. While the state debates each impossibility in turn, one man conducts a quieter audit — proving to himself, calculation by calculation, that of all the souls on Orison, only he can afford the unthinkable. The second Barycentric novel weighs a question the first only implied: what a good society does when goodness itself is the extinction event.
