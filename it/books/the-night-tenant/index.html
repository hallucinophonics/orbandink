Libro 1 di The Night Tenant

Overpopulation was solved by real-estate law applied to flesh: two licensed tenants per body — day shift and night shift, 08:00 to 20:00 and back — handed over by the Latch, a planetary broadcast that turns the whole world over at once. Co-tenants can never meet; sanctioned contact is a moderated message service; the note left on your own kitchen table is folk custom and a misdemeanor. Freehold is luxury, thirds are poverty, children are whole until eighteen. Every waking inherits the other tenant's fatigue — and lately, day-tenant Aldous Reyn is waking up with more than fatigue: calluses he didn't earn, a bruise with no history, a bulletin describing a decade he doesn't remember living. Alec Blanc's paranoid reality-thriller opens on the question that powers the trilogy: who, exactly, are you sharing yourself with?
