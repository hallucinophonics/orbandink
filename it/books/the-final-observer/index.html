Libro 3 di The Relic Protocol

Ten trillion years on, star formation is over and the sky is mostly embers and archives. The Vigil — a small, ancient civilisation of archive-keeper lineages — tends a banked white dwarf and curates the Gallery, the completest record of a universe going dark. Their creed: nothing should end unwitnessed. Their oldest standing order, written out of the case-law of ruined worlds, is the Relic Protocol. Tessera Malin, cartographer of dying laws, traces an inbound artefact to an origin outside the universe — and is chosen by the cult that carries it to hold the count of twelve dead worlds' dying wishes, because hers is the last mind clean enough to receive them, and to end them. The trilogy that began under a mountain closes at the last lit desk in existence.
