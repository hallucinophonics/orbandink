Livro 1 de Terms & Conditions

On a Tuesday in October, every screen on Earth displays the same document. It is 44,000 pages long, rendered in flawless local legalese, and courteous to a fault — which is the frightening part. Earth, it explains, is a manufactured product. The warranty on Production Batch 7,349,112 expires in ninety days, an assessment fleet is already en route, and humanity will be classified either DEFECTIVE — mandatory recall, humane, efficient, total — or ACCEPTABLE VARIANCE: warranty void, continued operation at owner's risk. The only person on Earth qualified to fight a claims-processing fleet is Rita Kessler, a burned-out Ohio insurance adjuster and the one human being who actually reads the fine print. She has ninety days, a yellow legal pad, and one professional conviction: everything hinges on the definitions section.
