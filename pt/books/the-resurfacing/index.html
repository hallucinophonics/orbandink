Livro 1 de The Drowned Houses

In the Amaranth Reach, the worlds bound to the Well, dynasties end by descending: patriarchs walk the Stair of Years toward the horizon, where a year of theirs is a century above, ruling their descendants through instructions that arrive generations late. Sinking is holy and one-way; surfacing is priced in current, the economy's spice; the Undertow creed teaches that the dead are not gone, only slow. Then the impossible: a transmission from House Vhast — the Tyrant house, deposed and driven down nine hundred years ago, presumed extinct when their monastery fell past the Threshold. It is dated, authenticated, and addressed by name to people not yet born when it was sent. The first of the Drowned Houses duology: dynastic gothic at relativistic depth, where every letter from the deep is both scripture and threat.
