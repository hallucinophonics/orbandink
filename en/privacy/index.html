Privacy

This site is a static catalogue of Alexey Belenkiy's books. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind. Every file it serves — pages, images, fonts — comes from this domain; nothing is loaded from third parties.

The pages are delivered by the hosting platform (Cloudflare Pages), which processes visitor connections — including IP addresses — at the network level, as any web host must to serve a page. The author receives no visitor logs and keeps none.

Buy and pre-order buttons lead to external bookstores. From the moment you follow one of those links, the store's own privacy policy applies.

If you write to the contact address, the message is used only to reply to you. Addresses are never added to lists, shared, or sold. To have past correspondence deleted, ask at the same address.
