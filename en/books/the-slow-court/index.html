Book 4 of The Seventh Watch

The Still is wrapped around the world's heart: frost gardens and lamplit terraces where time runs forty-to-one slow, a people awake since the beginning and only twenty generations old, keeping a book they can no longer read. A delegation climbs into that slowness with nine days of questions — while above them, the war those nine days contain burns through three years of fronts, harvests and betrayals. The strangest book of the sequence intercuts audience and apocalypse: protocol and decipherment at the Court of Hours, against campaigns whose seasons pass between one royal sentence and the next. After the Slow Court, there are no cosmological secrets left in the world — except the redacted page.
