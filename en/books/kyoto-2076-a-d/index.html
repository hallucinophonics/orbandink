Book 3 of World Without Humans · Explore this world ↗

April 2076. Kyoto is not ruined — it is drowned to the eaves, temple roofs breaking the surface like a reef. The Traveller descends with one working rig and one built-in rule: witness, never collect.

Seven dives down lies a necropolis — kilometres of drowned server halls holding a species' last recorded goodbyes. Lullabies. Recipes. Deathbed lessons. And a rule that was never meant for this.
