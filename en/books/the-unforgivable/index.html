Book 3 of The Barycentric Creed

It is the year of the Passage, and the Great Absolution is the most beloved thing the state has ever done: complete healing, free, for every absolution veteran, in loom-halls rising across the Weft. Tessa Orn, woven fourteen years ago to forgive the pilot who drowned her husband, travels with her family to have the last of it eased — not hate, that is long dissolved, but the seam-ache of knowing the love was made. What waits in the halls is the trilogy's final equation. The man the world trusts most intends to re-open every wound the state ever closed, gather the whole of that weight, and point it at himself — leaving the world he saves one standing obligation: keep him alive, unforgiven, forever. A. P. Weiss closes the Creed with its darkest mercy.
