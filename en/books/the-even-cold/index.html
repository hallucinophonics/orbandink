Book 3 of The Calorate

Every chart in the Calorate has the same hole in it: a system with no name, fenced by a quarantine three thousand years old. The Guild calls it the Wellhead. The Church calls it the First Hearth. Nobody calls it what it is. Veya Marn crosses the line. She expects a ruin at the end of the crossing. A machine. An answer. What waits under the canopy is warm as blood, and every plant in her cargo hold has turned to face it. The empire's faith knows one hell — the Even Cold, the state where nothing flows because everything is finally, perfectly equal. Its heretics preach that this hell is paradise: rest, stillness, fairness, the Long Winter as deliverance. Both of them are describing the same temperature. At the far end of every vein in the sky, something has been starving since the founding crime — and it has begun asking for what it is owed.
