Book 2 of The Universal Menu

Fourteen months after the landing, Earth is in full bloom: the cures work, the lifespans climb, the volunteer lotteries oversubscribe — and Max Reznik, the man who told his species the truth, is a stale meme. The Hospitality found his warning charming: a Master's passion for his ingredient honours the table. So when Earth's entry hearings convene at the Concourse, the seat of the Guild of Critics, Max goes — and finds, in the service corridors of alien politics, a rebellion nine centuries old against the eaters of worlds. He stakes Earth on it — with no army, no leverage and no credibility, only a stall, a knife, and a feud he can widen. The middle course of the Universal Menu is Bianco's satire at its sharpest: a book about reading the room, in a room where you are the menu.
