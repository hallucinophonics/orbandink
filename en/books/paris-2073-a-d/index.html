Book 2 of World Without Humans · Explore this world ↗

Paris, 2073. Gardens in the Métro, paintings hanging in a roofless Louvre, the Eiffel Tower leaning over a great silence. A traveller crosses a ruined world to answer one question — what ended it? — and finds that someone, long ago, laid a trail through the city for whoever was patient enough to follow.

Chamber by chamber, the trail leads toward an answer nobody left willingly.
