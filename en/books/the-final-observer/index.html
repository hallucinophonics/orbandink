Book 3 of The Relic Protocol

Ten trillion years on, star formation is over and the sky is mostly embers and archives. The Vigil — a small, ancient civilisation of archive-keeper lineages — tends a banked white dwarf and curates the Gallery, the completest record of a universe going dark. Their creed: nothing should end unwitnessed. Their oldest standing order, written out of the case-law of ruined worlds, is the Relic Protocol. Tessel Sorn, cartographer of dying laws, traces an inbound artefact to coordinates filed ten trillion years ago — and meets the fleet that has sailed for generations to bring it home. They are not mad. They are not lying. And they have come for her. The trilogy that began under a mountain closes at the last lit desk in existence.
