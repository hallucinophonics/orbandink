About the author

AUTHOR PORTRAIT

PLACEHOLDER — Alexey Belenkiy writes science fiction about time, memory, and the quiet machinery of power. His novels — the Meridian Loop cycle and the Ashfall Protocol dystopias — are published in eight languages.

PLACEHOLDER — He lives between two cities and several decades, and is currently at work on the final book of the Meridian Loop.
